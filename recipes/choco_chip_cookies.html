Chocolate Chip Cookie

Famous Neiman Marcus Recipe

You know you want one, or two, or several.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 3 tablespoons white sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla
  • 1&3/4 cup flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1&1/2 teaspoon instant espresso powder
  • 1&1/2 cup chocolate chips

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 300F, and cream the butter with sugars
    until fluffy.
  2. Beat in the egg and vanilla.
  3. Sift together dry ingredients, then beat into the wet
    ingredients. Gentily stir in the coffee powder and chocolate chips.
  4. Using a 1 ounce scoop, drop cookie dough onto a greased cookie sheet
    about 3 inches apart. Gently press down with the back of a spoon to spread
    into a 2 inch circle. Bake for 18-20 minutes or until golden brown.
